FEATURE ID: PRD-OWNLY-OPS-001

Two-Phase Machine Learning Predictive KPT Dispatch Engine

Status: Ready for Build

1. Problem Statement

Immediate dispatch of delivery captains upon order confirmation results in an average driver idle waiting time of 13.4 minutes outside busy restaurant kitchens. This costs captains ~28% in uncompensated time, increases daily cancellations by 14.2%, and creates crowd hazards at cloud kitchen entrances.

2. Algorithmic Architecture

The engine evaluates orders in two distinct lifecycle phases. In Phase 1, the order is ingested and passed through an XGBoost regressor evaluating active kitchen load, dish preparation complexity, and historical cook times. Dispatch broadcast is held back in a Kafka delay queue. In Phase 2, the dispatch trigger fires at T = KPT - Transit_ETA - 60s, matching a captain who arrives exactly as food packaging finishes.

3. Edge Case Mitigation

  • Kitchen Accelerates: If kitchen hits "Food Ready" early, system fires instant high-priority broadcast with ₹15 pickup bonus.
  • Captain Rejection: Cascades instantly to secondary matched captain within 800m with zero cooldown delay.
  • Monsoon Traffic Spike: Transit model recalculates dynamically; fires Phase 2 early if street speeds drop.

FEATURE ID: PRD-OWNLY-FLT-002

Modular Quick-Latch Pannier (QLP) Hardware & Mode-Switching OS

Status: Hardware Pilot

1. Physical Chassis Engineering

The Quick-Latch Pannier (QLP) solves the pillion seat conflict by utilizing an accordion-folding subframe mounted to the rear chassis behind the pillion grab rail. In Passenger Commute Mode, the side pods collapse to less than 8.5 cm, providing 100% foot-peg clearance for paying bike-taxi commuters. In Food Mode, a gas-strut quick release expands a 52-liter insulated cargo cell in 4.5 seconds.

2. Sensor & BLE Handshake

A high-durability magnetic reed switch embedded in the lock latch verifies mechanical deployment. The latch transmits an encrypted BLE ping to the Captain Android App. The server refuses food dispatch assignments unless the latch confirms HARDWARE_STATE: FOOD_CARGO.

FEATURE ID: PRD-OWNLY-DSG-003

Upfront Landed Price Engine & Transparent "Honest Bill" Funnel

Status: Production Design Frozen

1. Dismantling Dark Patterns

Conventional checkout funnels conceal packaging surcharges, rain fees, and the ₹17.58 platform fee until the final payment sheet, while pre-ticking tips and donations. OWNLY enforces strict upfront landed pricing: the price displayed on the search card is the exact final amount payable.

2. Core Funnel Mandates

  • Dine-In Price Guarantee: 100% price parity with in-store physical menus, certified by NRAI.
  • Capped Packaging: Zero packaging fee promise on all standard orders; no arbitrary ₹40 markups.
  • ₹0 Platform Fee: Permanently displayed in OWNLY Hot Pink with explicit zero-markup declaration.
  • Post-Delivery Tipping Only: Zero pre-ticked tips or unrequested charity contributions during checkout.

NRAI Merchant Portal & Unified Dynamic Menu Sync

Status: Ready for Integration

POS Ecosystem Integration

Direct bi-directional webhooks with India's leading restaurant POS systems: Petpooja, UrbanPiper, POSist, and DotPe. Restaurants maintain a single master menu; OWNLY automatically ingests changes with sub-second synchronization.

Automated Dine-In Parity Verifier

Diners can snap a photo of their physical dine-in bill inside any partner restaurant. If the physical bill proves a price lower than the OWNLY listed price, the diner earns an instant ₹100 bounty, and the restaurant is automatically flagged for contract review.
